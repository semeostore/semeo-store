ABOUT SEMEO

What is SEMEO?

SEMEO is a personal lifestyle brand created from the name Sameeh. Since 2022, SEMEO has focused on bringing customers a collection of shoes, watches, bags, gadgets, travel items and glasses.

With 5,000+ orders completed, SEMEO continues to build its identity around products, service and a simple online shopping experience.

SEMEO also has SEMEO FILMS, a creative division for wedding photography and videography.
