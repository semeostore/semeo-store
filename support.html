Shipping & Delivery

We’re committed to delivering your order safely and on time. We offer free delivery across Kerala. Orders outside Kerala are charged a flat ₹80 shipping fee. Kerala orders usually arrive within 1–2 business days, while deliveries to other states generally take 3–7 business days.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available across Kerala and other parts of India on selected products only.

For COD orders, a ₹150 advance payment is required to confirm the order. This ₹150 is a non-refundable COD handling charge paid towards the courier service and is separate from the product price.

For example, if your product price is ₹499, you will pay ₹150 online when placing the COD order, and the ₹499 product price will be paid at the time of delivery.

The ₹150 COD charge is non-refundable, including in cases of order cancellation, refusal, or failure to accept the delivery.

COD availability may vary depending on the product, location, and courier service. If COD is available for your selected product, the option will be provided while placing the order.

Return & Replacement Policy

We follow a strict No Refund & No Cancellation Policy. Replacement is available only for products that are damaged, defective, or incorrect.

A clear and complete unboxing video is mandatory for any replacement claim. The video must show the package from the sealed condition through the complete unboxing process. Without a proper unboxing video, replacement requests will not be accepted.

If you find any issue with your order, please inform us within 24 hours of delivery. Requests made after 24 hours or without proper unboxing proof will not be accepted.

  • The product must be packed securely and returned in new/unused condition.
  • The return must be sent through DTDC or India Post.
  • The sender’s complete address is mandatory.
  • The parcel must be shipped within 24 hours after we provide the return address.
  • Please send us a photo of the packed parcel and the tracking ID after shipping. Both are mandatory.
  • Please track your returned parcel and keep us updated until it reaches us. If the parcel is lost or missing after 7 days from shipping, SEMEO STORE will not be responsible for the loss.

Refund Policy

No refunds are provided. Eligible product issues will only be handled through the replacement process according to our Return & Replacement Policy.

Order cancellations are not accepted once the order has been confirmed. COD charges, including the ₹150 advance COD handling charge, are strictly non-refundable.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. We collect your personal information only when necessary to process your order, arrange delivery, provide customer support, and improve your shopping experience. We do not sell or misuse your personal information.
